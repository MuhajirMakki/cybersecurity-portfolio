# Home Network Asset Inventory

## Project Description

In this activity, I created an inventory of devices connected to a home network. The purpose was to identify important characteristics of each device and classify its sensitivity based on the type of information it contains and the possible impact if it is compromised.

I focused on three network-connected devices and documented their network access, owner, location, notes, and sensitivity level.

## Asset Inventory

| Asset                  | Network Access | Owner     | Location            | Notes                                                                                             | Sensitivity   |
| ---------------------- | -------------- | --------- | ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------- |
| External hard drive    | Occasional     | Homeowner | On-premises         | Contains personal files, music, and movies.                                                       | Confidential  |
| Streaming media player | Continuous     | Homeowner | On-premises         | Connected to the network for streaming. Payment card information may be stored for movie rentals. | Internal-only |
| Portable game console  | Occasional     | Friend    | On and off-premises | Connects to the home network and has a camera and microphone.                                     | Internal-only |

## Asset Classification

I classified each device according to the level of protection it requires.

* **External hard drive — Confidential:** It may contain personal files and other information that should not be accessible to unauthorized users.
* **Streaming media player — Internal-only:** It connects to the home network and may contain account or payment-related information.
* **Portable game console — Internal-only:** It connects to the network and includes devices such as a camera and microphone.

## What I Learned

This activity helped me understand how an asset inventory can be used to identify devices that need protection. The type of information stored on a device, how it connects to the network, who owns it, and where it is located can all affect its security classification.

Asset classification also helps determine the level of protection and access that should be applied to different devices.

## Cybersecurity Connection

Asset management is an important part of cybersecurity because an organization needs to know what assets it has before it can properly protect them.

For a security analyst, an asset inventory can help identify sensitive devices, understand possible entry points, and prioritize security controls based on risk.

## Conclusion

I created a basic home network asset inventory and classified three devices based on their characteristics and sensitivity. This activity gave me practice in identifying assets and thinking about confidentiality, integrity, and availability when evaluating security risks.
